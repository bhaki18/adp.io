About Me

Built from curiosity.

I'm Angelo Del Piano (ADP), a 17-year-old self-taught developer from Italy. What started as building browser games and interactive web pages quickly expanded into writing low-level C programs, Rust systems CLIs, local AI agents, and cross-platform desktop and mobile suites.

I love understanding how things work underneath the abstraction — whether that's projecting 3D triangles onto a 2D canvas without WebGL, packing files with LZ77 and CRC-32 checksums, caching manga pages directly in /dev/shm, or wiring an Arduino LED trigger.
